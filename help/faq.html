Frequently Asked Questions

Privacy

Can em access my data?

No. There is no em server at all — that's not a "em doesn't sell your data" promise, it's an architectural decision. Your writing syncs through your own iCloud account and never touches infrastructure em operates. Read more in em's Privacy Policy.

Files and iCloud

Why do libraries have to be in iCloud Drive?

iCloud is em's only sync feature. Your folders, your version history, your library settings — all of it reaches your other devices by living in an iCloud-synced folder.

What is Version History?

em keeps a record of changes made to your writing in case you ever need to restore an accidental change. Versions are saved automatically to em's folder inside of iCloud so they are separate from your actual writing.

To preserve iCloud space, Version History automatically cleans up after itself according to this schedule:

  • Versions less than 24 hours old are never touched.
  • Versions between one day and one week old are condensed to the most recent version per day — the rest are removed.
  • Versions older than a week are reduced to one per week — the rest go.

What is the .em folder inside my iCloud Drive?

This is em's home for preferences, Version Histories, and library customizations. It lives inside iCloud so it syncs across your devices. This folder can be deleted by performing a Factory Reset in Settings.

What happens to my writing if I stop using em?

Nothing. Your writing stays in its original iCloud location, exactly where it's always been.

I added a folder on one device. Why does my other device not add it automatically?

Each device needs to explicitly request permission to your iCloud libraries. This is a way to protect your files in accordance with Apple's policies.

Ghost

Why can't I edit my Ghost posts?

Ghost sites are read-only by default. To enable editing, turn this off in Settings.

Does em auto-publish my edits?

Never. Nothing reaches your site until you explicitly Save or choose to publish. If you leave a Ghost draft with unsaved changes, em asks what to do.

Does em support Ghost formats that are not part of Markdown?

Yes! em has a customized version of Markdown that supports 16 different Ghost card types, like callouts, image galleries, and toggles. This custom Markdown is denoted by ::: in the editor and can be used in local drafts too! Learn more about em's custom version of Markdown.

Can I use em to export posts from Ghost?

Yes. em gives you the ability to export a single post or your entire library as markdown files. This includes em's customized markdown format for Ghost-specific styles.

Search and the Index

Why do search results look wrong or stale?

It's possible something went wrong with your index. Don't worry, this is only an issue with em's index and your files are fine. Visit Settings → Index → Check Index Health to see what's going on. If needed, use Rebuild Search Index to recreate the index. The index is only a cache — rebuilding never touches your files.

Why does Smart Search say it's indexing?

Smart Search relies on semantic vectors ("chunks") that are built during search indexing. Creating chunks for each file in the library can take a few minutes, especially on large libraries. Each chunk is stored on-device in accordance with em's Privacy Policy. Once the index is finalized, Smart Search will be available.

AI

Do I have to use AI features?

Absolutely not! Your writing is yours and you should be the only one who decides what to do with it. This means you can use em without ever enabling its AI features. Markdown editing, Version History, Smart Search, and Ghost publishing all work without the need to enable AI.

Is my AI usage private?

This answer depends on which AI model you choose. When AI is enabled, em defaults to Apple Intelligence's on-device models. This means your questions and your data never leave your device. AI conversations, even with on-device models, are synced through iCloud so they appear in each version of em you use.

Using Editorial Review will send contents of the selected draft through remote servers for analysis. When the on-device model is selected, this happens using a Shortcut and Apple's Private Cloud Compute.

If a Remote Model (Claude, ChatGPT, or Gemini) is enabled, your questions and Editorial Review content are processed in accordance with their usage policies.

Read more about AI in em's Privacy Policy.

Who pays for using an external model?

You do. em connects using an API key you supply and usage is billed by the provider. You can revoke the API key from the provider's account or delete it from Keychain at any time. em is not a billing middleman and takes no cut on your AI usage.

The Danger Zone

What does Factory Reset do?

In Settings → Index there are a few erasure options. These settings only touch the data em creates — Version Histories, saved preferences, etc. Deleting these files will not touch your own Markdown files. Triggering a Factory Reset will revert em to a just-downloaded state on every device signed into your iCloud. This is an irreversible option. Even still, this action never touches your own files.

Odds and Ends

What's with the em dash highlighting?

The em dash is the single greatest punctuation mark there is and it's worth showing off — if you want, that is.

Can em read my writing aloud?

Yes. Read Aloud is in the Options menu (⋯), with your choice of voice and speed. It reads the whole article (or, on the Mac, just your selection) and skips code, tables, and images. Read Aloud is a good way to edit your writing based on what you hear.

Does em work offline?

Yes. So long as you download files from iCloud, they are available offline. Changes will sync the next time you connect to the internet. Smart Search, editing, Version History, and on-device AI all work offline. Ghost publishing and external models need the internet.
