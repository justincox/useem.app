Search and Smart Folders

em searches by keyword and by meaning. Ask for "the piece about software development" and Smart Search finds the essay you titled "that time I build an app" — because em understands what your drafts are about, not just which words they contain.

Two Kinds of Search

  • Search matches the words themselves — titles and full text, instantly, as you type.
  • Smart Search matches meaning. Switch to Smart Search from the search selector in the search field and describe what you're looking for — a topic, a half-remembered idea, even a question. em searches by meaning and keywords together, and orders results by what they're about. All of this happens completely on your device.

Smart Search doesn't require AI features. On a new install or right after a big import, the semantic index takes a little while to build, and Smart Search tells you so right in the search field. Keyword search works fully immediately.

Ghost posts are also searchable. Published posts are included by default; drafts and scheduled posts are included if you turn that on in Settings.

Smart Folders

A Smart Folder is a saved set of rules that stays current as your library changes:

  • Match any or all of your rules.
  • Rules can include title, content, library, favorite status, Ghost post status (draft, published, scheduled), or date the file was last edited.

Smart Folders live in the sidebar, sync to every device, and can be renamed and customized like any library.

Favorites

  • Favorite a document and it joins the Favorites list in the sidebar; the status travels with the folder to your other devices — Ghost posts can be favorites too.

Filter and Sort

Every file list can be narrowed and ordered:

  • The filter bar narrows the current list by title or favorite status as you type.
  • Sort by title, date created, date modified, or — for Ghost libraries — date published.
  • File rows can show text excerpts, dates, and file extensions — all of it is up to you in Settings.

When Search Seems Off

If results ever look stale or incomplete, Index Settings has the tools:

  • Check Index Health tells you what's wrong
  • Repair Index Metadata fixes the common cases
  • Rebuild Search Index starts everything over

The index is only a cache of your files — rebuilding it never touches your writing, and you never lose anything.
