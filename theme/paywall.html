Dark Mode Shouldn't Be an Afterthought

Every color in Paper & Ink is derived from the current ground at a fixed opacity — not hand-picked per mode.

Justin Cox
July 30, 2026 • 5 min read

Most themes pick two palettes and hand-tune each one until it looks right. Paper & Ink does something narrower: every panel, border, and muted text tone is color-mix()'d from a single ground color at a fixed opacity, so light and dark aren't two designs to maintain — they're one formula evaluated twice.
