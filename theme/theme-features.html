The Features of Paper & Ink

A sticky header, per-tag accent colors, a reading progress bar, related posts, post navigation — a tour of every setting Design → Brand gives you, and what it actually changes.

Justin Cox
August 28, 2026 • 4 min read

Paper & Ink ships with 17 settings under Settings → Design → Brand and design settings. None of them require touching a template. Here's what each group actually does.

Look & feel

🎨
Background (Light) / Background (Dark) set the two grounds the whole palette is derived from — every panel, border, and muted text tone is color-mix()'d from these at a fixed opacity, so changing one color updates the entire page consistently.

Accent (Secondary) and Accent (Success) add a second and third accent color used across links, labels, and callouts, alongside the primary accent Ghost already gives every theme.

Homepage

  • Show Feature Images in List — turns on post thumbnails in the homepage and tag list views (the same 100×100px grayscale-to-color thumbnail used on this demo's homepage).
  • Tag Color — post kickers and loop cards pick up each tag's own accent color instead of the theme accent.
  • Show Post Status plus the three status colors — a Free/Member/Paid label next to each post in list views, in whatever three colors you choose.

Reading a post

What does Sticky Header do?

Keeps the header pinned while scrolling, so search, subscribe, and navigation stay one click away no matter how far down the post you are.

Reading Progress Bar adds a scroll-progress indicator under the header on post pages — on by default. Post Navigation adds prev/next post links at the bottom of a post, and Related Posts shows a short list of related reading below that — both off by default, since not every site wants either.

Links & support

External Link Icons marks any link leaving your site with an accent underline and icon, on by default. Tip Url is the one setting with no Ghost-native equivalent: set any URL once, and a "Leave a Tip" link appears in both the support tile and the footer — Ghost has no built-in tip-jar mechanism, so this is yours to point anywhere you like. Copyright appends extra text to the footer's copyright line.

Every one of these is a toggle or a color picker — never a template edit.
