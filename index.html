Questions

Quick questions

The short answers. The long ones live in Help.

More in Help →
Do I need an account?

No. em has no server and no account. Your writing syncs through your own iCloud.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once your files are downloaded, em works without an iCloud or Ghost connection.

Do I have to use AI?

No. Every AI feature ships off, and search, indexing, and reading your files happen on your device either way.

What if I stop using em?

Your writing is exactly where it always was: a folder of Markdown files, with nothing missing and nothing to export.
