em's Features

Mac · iPad · iPhone

One app, every device

em works and syncs across the Mac, iPad, and iPhone so your writing is always available when inspiration strikes.

Built by a writer with tons of files who wanted better control

em's features all start from the same idea: your writing is yours, and it should stay that way.

Find anything you've ever written

Keyword and on-device semantic search — ask for "the piece about creating an app" and em finds it, even if you titled it something else.

Never lose a draft

Every edit is versioned automatically and synced across devices with iCloud. Compare any two versions, restore one, and never have accidental deletes ever again.

Publish straight to Ghost

Connect your Ghost site to fully edit, back up, and publish natively — including 16 custom card types, drafts, scheduling, and newsletters.

AI on your terms,
or not at all

You control if AI enters your workflow and what it accesses.

Off by default

Every AI feature comes disabled. Nothing runs, nothing is sent anywhere, until you turn it on.

On-device first

Ask em runs entirely on your Mac, iPad, or iPhone with Apple Intelligence — nothing leaves the device.

Always named

em says exactly which model is answering, every time, on every screen. No guessing what has access to your writing.

It's your writing, always

em has no backend to store data, no account to create, and no company database your writing could end up in. em and its developer cannot access anything you create, edit, or work with in the app. Ever.
